<threat_model>
ASVS L1 Threat Modeling for Plan 01-02:
- T-01-01 (Race Condition / Double Dispatch): Multiple responders concurrent accept, or single responder double-offered by two emergencies. Mitigation: Partial unique index `UNIQUE (responder_id) WHERE status IN ('OFFERED', 'ACCEPTED', 'ARRIVED')` and `UNIQUE (emergency_id, track) WHERE status IN ('OFFERED', 'ACCEPTED', 'ARRIVED')`.
- T-01-02 (Audit / Timeline Tampering): An attacker or malicious administrator attempts to modify or delete timeline events or audit logs to conceal unauthorized access. Mitigation: PostgreSQL `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` triggers unconditionally raising exceptions on `emergency_events` and `audit_logs`.
- T-01-03 (State Machine Corruption): Active emergency duplication per citizen. Mitigation: Partial unique index `UNIQUE (patient_user_id) WHERE status NOT IN ('COMPLETED', 'CANCELLED', 'NO_COVERAGE_CLOSED')`.
</threat_model>
