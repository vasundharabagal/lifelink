<threat_model>
ASVS L1 Threat Modeling for Plan 00-02:
- T-00-03 (Clickjacking / Frame Embedding): Malicious sites embed emergency screens in iframes to spoof actions. Mitigation: Enforce `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and CSP `frame-ancestors 'none'` in `vercel.json`.
- T-00-04 (MIME Sniffing & Script Execution): Browsers executing non-script MIME types as JavaScript. Mitigation: Enforce `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`.
- T-00-05 (Serverless Connection Exhaustion): Ephemeral function instances maintain idle connections, exhausting managed PostgreSQL pool. Mitigation: Enforce `NullPool` whenever `APP_ENV` is `production` or `preview`.
</threat_model>
